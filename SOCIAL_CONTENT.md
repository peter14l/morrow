**Title: I got tired of the "infinite scroll" trap, so I built an app that rewards you for focusing.**

Hey everyone, 

I’ve spent the last few months building an app called Oasis. I was feeling pretty burnt out by how most social platforms are basically engineered to keep us hooked, so I wanted to make a "digital sanctuary" instead.

A few things I baked into it:
- **Lock-in Mode:** You set a focus session (15-60 mins), and if you leave the app to check TikTok or whatever, you actually lose XP. It’s been helping me a lot with deep work.
- **Privacy that actually lasts:** Most E2EE won't stand up to future tech, so I implemented PQ-Aura (post-quantum encryption). Your journals and chats stay private forever.
- **Intentionality:** No algorithmic feeds. Just "Circles" for your close people and "Time Capsules" for memories you want to harvest later.

I’m really trying to make something that respects our time rather than stealing it. If you’re looking for a quieter corner of the internet, I’d love for you to check it out.
